This is why this year will be the year of startupsBlog

March 23, 2023

When it comes to jewellery, the experience of choosing the right piece is just as special as wearing it. After all, a diamond ring, pendant, or pair of earrings is a beautiful expression of your story. That's why Svaraa has reimagined how you shop for jewellery, making the process more personal, flexible, and meaningful. Whether you love the buzz of a store visit or the comfort of trying pieces at home, we offer experiences that suit every lifestyle.

As one of the most trusted trusted lab-grown diamond jewellery stores in India, we believe luxury shopping should never feel rushed or overwhelming. Instead, it should feel like an indulgent journey, where every step is customised to your needs. To make this possible, we offer three distinct try-on experiences: Online, In-store, and At-Home, ensuring that every customer finds their perfect sparkle in a way that feels just right.

Luxury Shopping Reimagined Online

Imagine shopping for diamonds without leaving your couch. With Svaraa's virtual jewellery appointment in India, that's exactly what you get. Our virtual try-on service allows you to connect with a dedicated jewellery expert over a video call, giving you a highly personalised consultation without stepping out of your home.

During the session, you can explore our collections in real-time, view intricate details of each piece, and ask every question you have, whether it's about cut, clarity, or styling advice. Think of it as having your own personal stylist who understands your preferences and helps you find jewellery that feels uniquely yours.

For those who value sustainability, this option is especially meaningful since you can choose from Svaraa's exquisite lab-grown diamond jewellery collections; pieces that carry both luxury and conscience.

Plan Your Visit, Shop at Ease

For those who still love the timeless charm of browsing jewellery in person. Svaraa's physical stores offer the perfect destination. With locations such as Ahmedabad - Shivranjani, Ahmedabad Sindhubhavan, Indore, Surat and Mumbai - Borivali, stepping into a Svaraa store is like stepping into a world of sparkle and craftsmanship.

The difference? Unlike traditional jewellery shopping that can often feel overwhelming, Svaraa ensures your in-store experience is calm, personal, and stress-free. By booking your visit in advance, you receive one-on-one guidance from our experts, who take the time to understand your preferences and walk you through designs at your pace.

Here, you can hold, feel, and try on each piece; whether it's an everyday essential or a statement jewel for a milestone celebration. And if you are dreaming of something that's one-of-a-kind, the custom design process begins right here, with our experts helping bring your vision to life.

Personalised Try-On at Your Doorstep

Now imagine this: instead of you going to the jewellery store, the jewellery comes to you. That's exactly what Svaraa's at-home try-on service offers. We bring a carefully curated selection of jewellery pieces right to your doorstep, letting you try them on in the comfort of your own space.

This experience is perfect for those who prefer privacy, need more time to make a decision, or simply want to see how the jewellery fits into their lifestyle before buying. You get to see how the sparkle catches the light in your living room, how a necklace pairs with your wardrobe, or how a ring feels during your daily routine.

Best of all, you will still have the guidance of Svaraa's experts, ensuring you feel confident about every choice you make. With only three operators required to make it happen, this service is as seamless and effortless as the diamonds themselves.
